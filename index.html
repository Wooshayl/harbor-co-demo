Four ways a project usually starts

Every job is different, but most enquiries fall into one of these — and it helps us prepare before we even speak.

Kitchens

Layout, joinery, worktops and the everyday details that decide whether a kitchen actually works.

Full home renovations

Structural, electrical, plumbing and finish, coordinated as one build from the first wall to the last coat.

Single rooms

A bedroom, bathroom or study done properly — smaller in scope, same level of attention.

Everything else

Extensions, outdoor spaces, one-off fixes. If it involves a house in Cork, tell us about it.

How a project runs

The same four stages, whether the job takes three weeks or eight months.

1

Tell us about it

A conversation — by chat, message or call — about scope, budget and timing, so we know if we're a fit.

2

A first look

We visit the property, or review photos and drawings, and talk through what's actually possible.

3

Design & detailing

Layouts, materials and finishes, refined with you until the plan is one you're confident in.

4

Build & handover

One team on site from start to finish, with a fixed point of contact throughout.
